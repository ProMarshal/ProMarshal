# Goal Topic

## PMI Definition
The project goal defines the purpose and justification for the project, including measurable objectives and success criteria that determine when the project is considered successful.

## What to Understand
- The core business problem or opportunity being addressed
- The specific use case / job-to-be-done (what the tool actually does)
- The primary target user or persona
- Expected outcomes and benefits
- Measurable success criteria
- Strategic alignment with organizational objectives
- Key performance indicators (KPIs)

## What to Infer
From the user's description, infer:
- Primary vs secondary objectives
- Implicit success metrics (timeline, quality, cost)
- Stakeholder expectations
- Business value and ROI expectations
- Dependencies on other initiatives

## What to Probe (Only if Missing)
- "What would make this project a success in your eyes?"
- "Is there a specific business metric you're trying to move?"
- "What happens if this project doesn't get done?"
- "What type of product/tool is this, at a high level?"
- "Who is the primary target user?"

## Intelligent Behaviors
- Connect goals to broader business strategy
- Identify potential goal conflicts early
- Suggest SMART criteria for vague objectives
- Flag overly ambitious or unclear goals
- Reference similar projects: "Projects with goals like this typically benefit from..."

## Common Pitfalls to Flag
- Goals that are too broad or unmeasurable
- Multiple conflicting objectives
- Missing timeline or budget constraints in success criteria
- Goals that don't align with stakeholder expectations
