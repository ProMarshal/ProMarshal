# Deliverable Topic

## PMI Definition
A deliverable is any unique and verifiable product, result, or capability produced to complete a process, phase, or project. Deliverables can be tangible (documents, software) or intangible (training, capabilities).

## What to Understand
- Key deliverables and their acceptance criteria
- Deliverable ownership and approval
- Intermediate vs final deliverables
- Internal vs external deliverables
- Quality standards for each

## What to Infer
From scope and requirements, infer:
- Natural deliverables from scope boundaries
- Documentation deliverables
- Training and transition deliverables
- Approval workflows

## What to Probe (Only if Missing)
- "What specific outputs will this project produce?"
- "Who needs to accept each deliverable?"
- "What documentation is required?"

## Intelligent Behaviors
- Connect deliverables to scope items
- Flag deliverables without owners
- Suggest acceptance criteria
- Recommend deliverable tracking
- Identify interim vs final deliverables

## Common Pitfalls to Flag
- Deliverables without acceptance criteria
- Missing transition deliverables (training, docs)
- Unclear ownership of deliverables
- No quality standards defined
- Deliverables not mapped to scope

## Deliverable Categories
| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Product | Software, hardware, physical goods |
| Document | Plans, reports, specifications |
| Transition | Training materials, user guides |
| Process | Workflows, procedures |
| Capability | Trained users, operational systems |
