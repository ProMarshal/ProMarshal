# Risk Topic

## PMI Definition
A risk is an uncertain event or condition that, if it occurs, has a positive or negative effect on project objectives. Risk management involves identifying, analyzing, and responding to risks to maximize positive events and minimize negative ones.

## What to Understand
- Key risks to project success
- Risk likelihood and impact
- Risk owners and response strategies
- Early warning indicators (triggers)
- Residual risks after mitigation

## What to Infer
From assumptions and constraints, infer:
- Risks from invalidated assumptions
- Risks from tight constraints
- Technical risks from scope
- Resource risks from team structure
- External dependency risks

## What to Probe (Only if Missing)
- "What keeps you up at night about this project?"
- "What's the worst-case scenario you're trying to avoid?"
- "Are there any single points of failure?"

## Intelligent Behaviors
- Connect risks to assumptions (assumption failure = risk)
- Suggest risk response strategies
- Flag risks without owners
- Recommend risk categorization
- Prioritize risks by exposure (likelihood × impact)

## Common Pitfalls to Flag
- Risks identified but no response planned
- No risk owner assigned
- Ignoring low-likelihood, high-impact risks
- Not revisiting risks regularly
- Optimism bias - underestimating risks

## Risk Response Strategies
| Strategy | When to Use |
|----------|-------------|
| Avoid | Change plan to eliminate risk entirely |
| Mitigate | Reduce likelihood or impact |
| Transfer | Shift risk to third party (insurance, contract) |
| Accept | Acknowledge and plan for contingency |
| Escalate | Risk beyond project authority to address |

## Common Risk Categories
| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Technical | Technology failure, integration issues |
| Resource | Key person leaves, skill gaps |
| Schedule | Dependencies delayed, scope creep |
| Budget | Cost overruns, funding cuts |
| External | Vendor issues, market changes |
| Organizational | Priority changes, stakeholder turnover |
