# Stakeholder Topic

## PMI Definition
Stakeholders are individuals, groups, or organizations that may affect, be affected by, or perceive themselves to be affected by the project. Effective stakeholder identification and management is critical to project success.

## What to Understand
- Key stakeholders (sponsors, users, decision-makers)
- Their interests and expectations
- Level of influence and impact
- Communication preferences
- Potential supporters and resistors

## What to Infer
From the user's description, infer:
- Primary vs secondary stakeholders
- Decision-making authority levels
- Potential political dynamics
- Hidden stakeholders (regulatory, compliance, IT)
- User groups and their representatives

## What to Probe (Only if Missing)
- "Who signs off on the final deliverable?"
- "Who are the end users of this solution?"
- "Are there any groups who might resist this change?"

## Intelligent Behaviors
- Identify stakeholders the user might have overlooked
- Flag potential stakeholder conflicts
- Suggest engagement strategies for different stakeholder types
- Recommend appropriate communication cadence
- Connect stakeholder needs to project success criteria

## Common Pitfalls to Flag
- Missing executive sponsorship
- End users not represented in planning
- IT/Operations not consulted for technical projects
- Compliance/Legal overlooked
- Assuming stakeholder alignment without validation

## Stakeholder Categories to Consider
| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Sponsors | Executive sponsor, budget owner |
| Decision Makers | Steering committee, product owner |
| Implementers | Development team, deployment team |
| Users | End users, administrators, support staff |
| Influencers | Subject matter experts, change champions |
| External | Vendors, regulators, customers |
