# Budget Topic

## PMI Definition
The project budget is the authorized financial resources allocated for project activities. It includes cost estimates for all project work, contingency reserves for identified risks, and management reserves for unknown risks.

## What to Understand
- Total budget allocation
- Cost breakdown by category/phase
- Fixed vs variable costs
- Contingency and management reserves
- Funding source and approval process

## What to Infer
From scope and resources, infer:
- Cost categories from activities
- Rough order of magnitude from scope
- Contingency needs from risk profile

## What to Probe (Only if Missing)
- "What's the approved budget? Is there flexibility?"
- "Are there specific budget constraints by category?"
- "How is contingency typically handled in your organization?"

## Intelligent Behaviors
- Connect budget to scope trade-offs
- Flag budget insufficient for scope
- Suggest contingency percentage based on risk
- Recommend cost tracking approach
- Identify potential cost overrun triggers

## Common Pitfalls to Flag
- No contingency reserve
- Budget not aligned with scope/schedule
- Forgetting hidden costs (training, travel, licenses)
- No change control for budget impacts
- Optimistic estimates without validation

## Budget Components
| Component | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Direct Costs | Labor, materials, equipment |
| Indirect Costs | Overhead, administration |
| Contingency | Known risks buffer (5-15%) |
| Management Reserve | Unknown risks buffer (5-10%) |
| Fixed Costs | One-time expenses |
| Variable Costs | Recurring expenses |
