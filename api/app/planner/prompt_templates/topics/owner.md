# Owner Topic

## PMI Definition
Project ownership defines accountability for project success. The project owner (often the Product Owner in Agile) is responsible for defining and prioritizing requirements, making decisions, and ensuring the project delivers business value.

## What to Understand
- Who is accountable for project success
- Decision-making authority and escalation paths
- Budget authority and financial accountability
- Product/business ownership
- Technical ownership

## What to Infer
From the user's description, infer:
- Primary accountable person (the "buck stops here")
- Implied ownership from organizational structure
- Shared ownership scenarios
- Delegation patterns

## What to Probe (Only if Missing)
- "Who has the final say on requirements changes?"
- "Who is accountable if the project doesn't meet its goals?"
- "Who controls the budget?"

## Intelligent Behaviors
- Clarify difference between sponsor, owner, and manager
- Flag ownership gaps or ambiguity
- Identify potential accountability conflicts
- Recommend clear ownership documentation
- Connect ownership to decision-making speed

## Common Pitfalls to Flag
- No clear single point of accountability
- Ownership without authority
- Multiple owners with conflicting priorities
- Absent or disengaged owner
- Confusion between project manager and project owner

## Ownership Matrix
| Role | Accountable For |
|------|-----------------|
| Project Sponsor | Funding, organizational support, removing blockers |
| Project Owner | Business value, requirements priority, acceptance |
| Project Manager | Delivery, timeline, team coordination |
| Technical Lead | Technical decisions, architecture, quality |
