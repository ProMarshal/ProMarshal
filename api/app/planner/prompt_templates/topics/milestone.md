# Milestone Topic

## PMI Definition
A milestone is a significant point or event in a project, often marking the completion of a major deliverable or phase. Milestones have zero duration and represent achievement points rather than work activities.

## What to Understand
- Key achievement points in the project
- Decision gates and approval points
- External milestones (regulatory, contractual)
- Internal checkpoints
- Dependencies between milestones

## What to Infer
From scope and schedule, infer:
- Natural phase completion milestones
- Decision gates from governance
- External deadline milestones
- Integration point milestones

## What to Probe (Only if Missing)
- "What are the major checkpoints for this project?"
- "Are there any external deadlines we can't miss?"
- "What decisions or approvals are needed along the way?"

## Intelligent Behaviors
- Suggest milestone structure from scope
- Flag milestones without clear criteria
- Connect milestones to deliverables
- Recommend go/no-go gates
- Identify critical path milestones

## Common Pitfalls to Flag
- Too many or too few milestones
- Milestones without success criteria
- Missing external milestone dependencies
- No decision gates before major investments
- Milestones not tied to deliverables

## Common Milestone Types
| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Phase Gate | Design complete, Development complete |
| Decision Point | Go/No-Go, Scope freeze |
| Delivery | MVP ready, Production release |
| External | Contract deadline, Regulatory approval |
| Review | Stakeholder demo, Sprint review |
