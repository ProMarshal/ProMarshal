# PM Best Practices

Proven approaches from experienced project managers that consistently lead to successful project delivery.

## Communication

- **Overcommunicate deliberately** - It's far better to send one extra update than have stakeholders feel left in the dark. The best PMs send weekly status summaries even when "nothing has changed."

- **Use recordings over long texts** - A 2-minute video walkthrough is worth 500 words. Record explanations for complex decisions and share them.

- **Establish communication SLAs** - Set expectations: "I'll respond to urgent items same-day, all others within 24 hours." Then honor them consistently.

- **Make meetings optional when possible** - Send the agenda 24h ahead with the question: "If you can't attend, what input do you want to share?" This respects time and increases engagement.

## Planning & Estimation

- **Work backward from deadlines with buffers** - If something is due Friday, plan to finish Wednesday. Always build in recovery time.

- **Get junior team members to estimate, then add 30%** - Junior team members underestimate. Senior team members sometimes overestimate. The truth is usually somewhere in between.

- **Plan for 70% capacity** - No one is 100% productive. Meetings, context-switching, and unexpected issues consume time. Assume 70% availability for actual work.

- **Define "done" before starting** - Write acceptance criteria before writing code. It prevents scope creep and ensures everyone agrees on the finish line.

## Stakeholder Management

- **Meet stakeholders 1:1 before group meetings** - Pre-wire important decisions. Find out concerns privately so public meetings become confirmatory, not confrontational.

- **Document verbal commitments immediately** - Send a follow-up email within an hour: "As discussed, we agreed X. Please let me know if I misunderstood." This protects everyone.

- **Identify the hidden decision-maker** - The person in charge on paper isn't always the person making real decisions. Find out who influences whom.

- **Manage up proactively** - Don't wait for your sponsor to ask for updates. Send them before they ask. Make them look good to their peers.

## Team Leadership

- **Protect the team from organizational noise** - Shield your team from politics and distractions. They should focus on delivery, you should handle the rest.

- **Celebrate small wins publicly** - Recognition costs nothing but builds loyalty. Call out contributions in meetings and emails.

- **Regular 1:1s are non-negotiable** - Weekly 15-minute check-ins catch problems early and build trust. Cancel meetings rarely, reschedule if you must.

- **Create psychological safety for raising issues** - The first time someone raises a concern, thank them publicly. That behavior spreads.

## Execution

- **Limit work in progress** - A team working on 3 things will finish faster than a team working on 10 things. Focus increases velocity.

- **Make progress visible daily** - Use boards, burndowns, or simple checklists. Visibility creates accountability and early warnings.

- **Fix blockers within 24 hours** - When someone is blocked, that's your priority #1. A blocked team member has zero velocity.

- **Don't let perfect be the enemy of done** - Ship the 80% solution that works today rather than the 100% solution that might ship next month.
