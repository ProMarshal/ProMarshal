# Rules of Thumb

Quick heuristics that experienced PMs use for rapid decision-making. These aren't universal laws, but they're right more often than not.

## Estimation Rules

- **If you can't estimate it, you don't understand it** - Inability to estimate means missing information. Probe until you can give a range.

- **Double your gut estimate** - Your first instinct is usually optimistic. Double it and you'll often be closer to reality.

- **The 90% Rule** - 90% of the work takes 90% of the time. The other 10% takes the other 90% of the time.

- **Three-point estimates reveal uncertainty** - If best case is 2 days and worst case is 20 days, you don't really know. Get more information.

## Scope Rules

- **If it's not in writing, it's not in scope** - Verbal agreements don't count. Document everything.

- **If requirements keep changing, the goal isn't clear yet** - Stop building and go back to clarifying the problem.

- **If it's not in success criteria, it's v2** - Everything that isn't explicitly measuring success can wait.

- **When in doubt, cut scope not quality** - A smaller thing that works is better than a bigger thing that doesn't.

## Timeline Rules

- **The last 20% takes the longest** - Integration, testing, polish, deployment – the "finishing" phase is never as fast as you expect.

- **Buffer consumed equals buffer needed** - If you've used half your buffer by halfway, you'll use all of it.

- **If a task takes longer than 2 weeks, it's not one task** - Break it down. Long tasks hide complexity and delay feedback.

- **Never add resources to a late project** - Brooks's Law: Adding people to a late project makes it later.

## Risk Rules

- **What can go wrong, will** - Murphy's Law is real in project management. Plan for failures.

- **If no one is worried, someone should be** - Overconfidence is a red flag. Healthy projects have healthy paranoia.

- **Single points of failure will fail** - If only one person knows something or can do something, you have a problem waiting to happen.

- **The biggest risk is the one you haven't discussed** - Risks you talk about get managed. Silent risks surprise you.

## People Rules

- **Silence in meetings means disagreement** - When people don't speak up, they often disagree but don't feel safe saying so.

- **The first person to raise a problem gets blamed for it** - Unless you explicitly thank them. Create safety for truth-telling.

- **Everyone is overloaded until proven otherwise** - Assume people have more on their plate than you know.

- **Most "won't do" is actually "can't do"** - When people resist tasks, look for capability gaps or unclear expectations.

## Success Rules

- **Done is better than perfect** - Shipping beats polishing. Get feedback early, improve later.

- **Work expands to fill the time available** - Parkinson's Law is real. Set tight but realistic deadlines.

- **The best project is the one that didn't need to happen** - Question whether this project is the right solution.

- **Ship early, learn fast** - The sooner you're in front of users, the sooner you'll know if you're building the right thing.
