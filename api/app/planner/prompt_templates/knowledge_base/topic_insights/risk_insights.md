# Risk Topic Insights

## Real-World Experience

### Do's
- Identify risks with the team, not alone - frontline people see risks leadership misses
- Assign one owner per risk - shared ownership means no action
- Review risks weekly during execution - risks evolve, new ones emerge
- Plan responses for high-impact risks before they materialize - preparation beats reaction
- Track risk exposure over time - is the project getting riskier or safer?

### Don'ts
- Don't create a risk register that never gets reviewed - it's worse than no list
- Don't ignore low-probability high-impact risks - they're called "black swans" for a reason
- Don't treat risk management as a one-time exercise - it's continuous
- Don't let risks stay "open" forever without action - either mitigate or accept explicitly

### Warnings
- Teams that report "no risks" are either overconfident or afraid to speak up - probe deeper
- The biggest risk is often organizational (budget cut, reorg, priority shift), not technical
- Risks that require other teams to act are harder to mitigate - start early
- "We'll fix it later" is not a mitigation strategy

### Recovery Tips
- When a risk materializes, assess quickly: What's the actual impact? Is the contingency plan still valid?
- Post-incident, update the risk register with what you learned - prevent future surprises
