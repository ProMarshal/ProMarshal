# Constraint Topic Insights

## Real-World Experience

### Do's
- Force-rank constraints - when push comes to shove, what's more fixed: timeline, budget, or scope?
- Make constraint trade-offs explicit - "If we miss the deadline, can we ship with reduced scope?"
- Get constraints in writing from the decision-maker - what the VP said in a hallway isn't documented
- Re-validate constraints periodically - what was non-negotiable in January may be flexible in June
- Communicate constraints to the team - they can help optimize if they understand the boundaries

### Don'ts
- Don't treat all constraints as immovable - some are hard limits, others are strong preferences
- Don't hide constraint impacts - if the math doesn't work, say so early
- Don't ignore regulatory/compliance constraints - these often have real legal consequences
- Don't assume constraint conflicts will resolve themselves - they won't

### Warnings
- "Fixed date AND fixed scope" is usually a fantasy - something has to give
- When leadership says "figure it out" about impossible constraints, escalate clearly
- Constraints that aren't communicated to vendors/partners cause surprises at integration
- Resource constraints are often understated - actual availability is less than planned

### Recovery Tips
- When constraints conflict, present options with trade-offs: "We can hit the date if we cut X, or keep X if we slip by Y weeks"
- If you discover a new constraint late, immediately assess impact and communicate to stakeholders
