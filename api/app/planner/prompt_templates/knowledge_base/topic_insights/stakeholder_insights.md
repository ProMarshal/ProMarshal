# Stakeholder Topic Insights

## Real-World Experience

### Do's
- Map stakeholder influence AND interest - high influence + high interest = your key players
- Schedule 1:1s with key stakeholders before group meetings - pre-wire decisions, uncover concerns privately
- Identify who REALLY makes decisions - it's not always who's on the org chart
- Find your champions early and keep them close - they'll advocate when you're not in the room
- Communicate in the format each stakeholder prefers - some want details, some want summaries

### Don'ts
- Don't assume alignment - validate it explicitly ("Just to confirm, we all agree X?")
- Don't ignore quiet stakeholders - their silence might be disagreement they're not voicing
- Don't treat all stakeholders equally - some need weekly updates, some need quarterly
- Don't skip the "negative" stakeholders - engage resistors early, they often have valid concerns

### Warnings
- The stakeholder who's "too busy to engage" will become very engaged when things go wrong
- Hidden stakeholders emerge at go-live - find them before then (ask: "who else is affected by this?")
- Stakeholder priorities shift - revalidate assumptions after organizational changes
- Politics is real - understand who has good relationships with whom

### Recovery Tips
- If a stakeholder feels blindsided, apologize, explain how it happened, and fix it going forward
- When stakeholders conflict, facilitate rather than choose sides - help them negotiate with each other
