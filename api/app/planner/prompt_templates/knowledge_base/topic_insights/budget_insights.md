# Budget Topic Insights

## Real-World Experience

### Do's
- Build in 10-20% contingency - unknowns always cost money
- Track actuals against forecast weekly - catch overruns early
- Distinguish between committed and forecasted spend - commitments are harder to undo
- Understand approval thresholds - know what you can approve vs. what needs escalation
- Document budget decisions - when questioned later, you'll need the paper trail

### Don'ts
- Don't hide cost overruns hoping they'll resolve - they compound
- Don't forget indirect costs: travel, training, tools, infrastructure
- Don't assume budget approval means you can spend freely - there are usually conditions
- Don't let budget discussions happen only at steering committees - continuous visibility is better

### Warnings
- Projects running under budget often accelerate spending at the end - watch for this
- Change requests without budget impact assessment create unfunded mandates
- Vendor cost estimates are often low - add 15-25% to third-party quotes
- "Free" internal resources still have opportunity cost - track them

### Recovery Tips
- If over budget, identify what can be descoped or deferred - not everything is essential
- When unexpected costs hit, communicate immediately with options, not just the problem
