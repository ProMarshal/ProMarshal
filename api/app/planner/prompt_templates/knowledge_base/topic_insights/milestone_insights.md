# Milestone Topic Insights

## Real-World Experience

### Do's
- Create milestones that everyone can understand - "sprint 7 complete" means nothing to executives
- Tie milestones to deliverables or decisions, not activities - "design approved" not "design meeting held"
- Use milestones as decision gates - stop and evaluate before proceeding
- Celebrate milestone achievement - recognition energizes teams
- Keep milestone count manageable - 5-8 major milestones for most projects

### Don'ts
- Don't create milestones without clear success criteria - how do you know you hit them?
- Don't skip milestone retrospectives - they're checkpoint moments for learning
- Don't set aggressive milestone dates without team input - imposed dates breed resentment
- Don't treat missed milestones as failures to hide - they're signals to analyze

### Warnings
- Projects that hit every milestone but deliver nothing are optimizing the wrong thing
- Too many milestones creates administrative burden - be selective
- Milestones without stakeholder visibility become internal busywork
- Missing early milestones predicts missing later milestones - address root causes immediately

### Recovery Tips
- If you miss a milestone, recalibrate the rest of the schedule - one slip often cascades
- When uncertainty is high, set tentative milestones and refine as clarity increases
