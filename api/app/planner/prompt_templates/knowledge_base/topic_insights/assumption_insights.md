# Assumption Topic Insights

## Real-World Experience

### Do's
- Document assumptions at project start - they're often forgotten once work begins
- Flag high-risk assumptions that could invalidate the plan - prioritize validating these early
- Revisit assumptions monthly - circumstances change, assumptions go stale
- Treat each assumption as a potential risk - if assumption X fails, what happens?
- Get confirmation on key assumptions from people who would know - don't assume alone

### Don'ts
- Don't treat assumptions as facts - they're explicitly things you believe but haven't validated
- Don't assume availability of shared resources - verify with their manager
- Don't assume technology works as advertised - prototype first
- Don't let assumptions live only in your head - write them down

### Warnings
- "Everyone knows that" usually means no one has actually verified it
- The most dangerous assumptions are the ones so obvious no one states them
- When an assumption fails mid-project, the impact is usually larger than expected
- Assumptions about stakeholder support are frequently wrong

### Recovery Tips
- When an assumption fails, quickly assess which parts of the plan are affected
- Create contingency plans for your top 3 riskiest assumptions before you need them
