# Goal Topic Insights

## Real-World Experience

### Do's
- Write goals that can be measured by someone outside the project - vague goals benefit no one
- Include timeline in success criteria - "increase revenue" is weak; "increase revenue 15% by Q4" is actionable
- Limit to 3-5 primary goals - more than that and nothing is really a priority
- Frame goals as outcomes, not activities - "reduce churn" not "implement retention features"
- Revisit goals monthly - what mattered at kickoff may not matter 6 months later

### Don'ts
- Don't confuse deliverables with goals - "ship mobile app" is a deliverable; "expand mobile user base 40%" is a goal
- Don't create goals you can't measure - if you can't prove success, you can't prove failure either
- Don't set goals that conflict with each other - "fastest to market" and "highest quality" often fight
- Don't keep goals secret - if the team doesn't know the goal, they can't work toward it

### Warnings
- "Strategic alignment" discussions that never end are a sign no one actually owns the decision
- When every goal is "P0" priority, none of them are - force ranking reveals what really matters
- Goals that only leadership understands but can't explain simply aren't clear enough

### Recovery Tips
- If goals feel fuzzy, ask: "How will we know we succeeded?" - if no one can answer, the goal isn't ready
- When stakeholders can't agree on goals, facilitate a "if we could only achieve ONE thing" exercise
