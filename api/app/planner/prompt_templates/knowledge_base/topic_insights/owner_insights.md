# Owner Topic Insights

## Real-World Experience

### Do's
- Establish one clear "buck stops here" person at kickoff - shared accountability often means no accountability
- Distinguish between owner (accountable) and stakeholders (informed) - don't let everyone feel like an owner
- Ensure owner has authority to match their accountability - responsibility without power is a setup for failure
- Get owner actively engaged, not just named - absent owners create vacuums
- Document decision rights explicitly - who can approve what, up to what amount/impact

### Don'ts
- Don't proceed without clear ownership - ambiguity here causes delays and conflicts
- Don't confuse the PM with the owner - PM manages the project, owner owns the outcome
- Don't let sponsors be absent - if they're too busy to engage, the project isn't important enough
- Don't assume ownership is obvious - people interpret titles differently

### Warnings
- "Everyone owns it" means no one owns it - force a single point of accountability
- When the owner delegates everything, they've abdicated - escalate if this creates problems
- Owner changes mid-project are disruptive - build relationships also with backup contacts
- Owners who only appear for status meetings aren't engaged enough - get them more involved

### Recovery Tips
- If ownership is genuinely shared, create clear "swim lanes" - each person owns a specific slice
- When owner decisions are slow, propose a timeboxed default: "I'll proceed with X unless I hear otherwise by Friday"
